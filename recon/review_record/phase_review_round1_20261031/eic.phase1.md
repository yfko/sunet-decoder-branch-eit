## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor, mandatory, methodology seat only). From the editor's chair this dimension asks whether the study was built and reported well enough that a competent peer could trust the numbers: a design that answers the registered question, data handling and statistics reported to the field's standard, and enough reproducibility material (code, seeds, hashes, data) that the result could be regenerated. I do not score it; I only need to know that a block here from the methodology seat is a mandatory-dimension event the synthesizer will weigh above anything I report on structure.

D2 (domain_accuracy, mandatory, domain seat only). This dimension asks whether what the manuscript says about its own field is true: prior work represented as it actually stands, terminology used correctly, and reported results not contradicting established domain evidence. It belongs to the domain seat. For my oversight purpose it matters because a venue judged on technical soundness cannot carry a factual misstatement about the literature even when the experiment itself is sound.

D3 (argumentative_coherence, mandatory, devil's-advocate seat with methodology co-eligible). This dimension asks whether the paper's central line of reasoning holds together from question to conclusion: each claim rests on evidence actually presented, the confirmatory and exploratory parts are not silently merged into one story, and no fallacy carries the main inference. I do not score it, but the same evidence surfaces (abstract, conclusions, registered rules) that the DA reads for coherence are the ones I read for venue fit, so I expect overlap in anchors without overlap in verdicts.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance, high priority, perspective seat only). This dimension asks whether a reader from a neighbouring field can follow the framing, definitions and implications without insider knowledge, and whether any claim that reaches across disciplines is actually substantiated rather than gestured at. It is not mine to score. It is high-priority rather than mandatory, so a block here moves the decision to major revision but cannot by itself reject.

D5 (writing_and_structure, normal priority, my seat). This is the editor's reading-experience dimension: is the manuscript organised so that a reviewer and a reader can find the question, the registered rules, the results that answer them and the conclusions in the places the venue's conventions put them; is the exposition clear; do tables, figures and captions stand on their own and agree with the text; does the manuscript follow the venue's structural and referencing conventions. It is non-mandatory, so its worst outcome feeds F5 (minor revision) and never a fatal.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution, mandatory, my seat). This is the editor's gatekeeping dimension: does this manuscript belong at the configured venue and does it deliver a contribution that venue's readers can use. For a venue whose publication criteria are technical soundness and conclusions supported by the data rather than novelty or significance, "contribution" must be read as a sound, clearly scoped, honestly reported answer to a stated question, not as a claim of field-changing importance; demanding novelty here would apply the wrong venue's bar. The dimension is mandatory, so a block from me triggers F2 and a fatal triggers F1.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure
dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: IMRaD order with the registered hypotheses, decision rules and their outcomes locatable in predictable places; every number that appears in abstract or text matching the corresponding table or figure value; table and figure captions self-contained and consistent with how the text describes them; Vancouver citation numbering sequential and consistent between text and reference list; Methods written so a reader does not need an external preregistration file to understand what was run; main-text length and section inventory consistent with the venue's research-article conventions; deviations from the registered protocol reported in a findable place rather than scattered.
what_triggers_block: a numeric value stated in the abstract, text or figure caption that contradicts the value for the same quantity in a table or figure, or an organisation so disordered that the registered hypotheses and the results answering them cannot be located without reconstructing the study from fragments.
what_triggers_warn: captions that cannot be read without the surrounding text, citation numbering or reference-list formatting that departs from the venue's Vancouver convention in more than isolated instances, Methods that defer essential procedural detail to an external document, or a main text whose length clearly exceeds venue norms without a stated reason.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution
dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: whether the title, abstract and conclusions claim exactly what the registered decision rules and reported results support, no wider and no narrower; whether the confirmatory preregistered findings and the two post-unblinding addenda are kept in separate standing in the abstract and conclusions, with the addenda labelled as exploratory or hypothesis-generating; whether the fixed interpretation sentences that were registered are used as written when their trigger condition is met; whether the contribution offered to readers is a sound, scoped answer to the stated question, which is the configured venue's actual bar, rather than a novelty or significance claim; whether the manuscript addresses the venue's readership of practitioners who would reuse the method or avoid the pitfall it documents.
what_triggers_block: the abstract or conclusions present a post-unblinding addendum result with the same confirmatory standing as a preregistered result, or state a conclusion that is broader in scope (population, reconstructor, noise regime, task) than the registered decision rules and reported results license, or omit a registered fixed interpretation sentence whose trigger condition the reported results meet.
what_triggers_warn: Discussion-only overreach that the abstract and conclusions do not repeat, a venue-fit statement or contribution framing that leans on novelty or significance language the venue does not require and the data do not support, or a deviation from the registered protocol acknowledged in the text but not accounted for in how the conclusions are worded.
what_triggers_fatal: the headline claim carried by the title or abstract is the opposite of, or unsupported by, the outcome that the manuscript's own registered decision rule produces on the manuscript's own reported numbers, so that a technically-sound-and-supported venue could not publish the paper under its current title and abstract without the central claim being rewritten.
