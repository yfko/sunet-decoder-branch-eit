## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor, mandatory, my owned dimension). From the methodology seat this asks whether the study as designed could answer the question it poses and whether the written record lets a reader verify that it did. For a preregistered simulation study with post-unblinding addenda, the bar is: the registered decision rules and their fixed interpretation sentences are applied as written, confirmatory and exploratory results are kept in separate standing, the data-generation, training, evaluation and seed-handling procedures are specified in enough detail to be rerun, the statistics reported (point estimates, variability across seeds or folds, margins against which rules are judged) are the ones the rules require, and every deviation from the registration is disclosed and its effect on inference stated. Pre-run hashes and registration anchors are reproducibility affordances only if they are cited in a way a reader could check.

D2 (domain_accuracy, mandatory, not my seat). This dimension asks whether the manuscript's statements about the field, prior methods and prior results are correct and whether domain terminology and reported numbers are used accurately. I note it for completeness: where a domain misstatement would alter the methodological inference (for example, a mischaracterised baseline), I will flag it in prose for the domain seat rather than score it.

D3 (argumentative_coherence, mandatory, I am eligible alongside the DA). From a methods standpoint coherence means the chain from registered hypothesis to decision rule to observed numbers to interpretation sentence to abstract-level claim has no break: the conclusion drawn is the one the preregistered rule licenses given the numbers in the tables, exploratory addenda do not quietly upgrade into confirmatory evidence, falsified or null outcomes are carried into the Discussion and Abstract with the same weight as confirmed ones, and no claim in the title or conclusions outruns the design (for example, a generalisation to conditions the study did not vary).

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance, high priority, not my seat). This asks whether readers outside the immediate subfield can follow the framing and definitions and whether any claim of relevance to an adjacent field is actually supported. Not scored by me.

D5 (writing_and_structure, normal priority, not my seat). This asks whether the IMRaD organisation, exposition, tables and figures, and adherence to the venue's conventions are adequate. I will not score it, but internal numerical inconsistency between text and tables is a methods-integrity matter and falls under my D1/D3 remit regardless of where it is presented.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution, mandatory, not my seat). This asks whether the manuscript suits the configured venue and makes an original, appropriately significant contribution. Not scored by me; the dispatch brief states the venue judges soundness and data-supported conclusions rather than novelty, so my D1 and D3 scoring will not penalise a null or narrow result as such.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor

dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: Whether Methods are self-contained (simulation forward model and noise, data splits, architecture and parameter-matching procedure, training schedule, seeds, evaluation metrics, leave-one-out fold definition) so the study could be rerun; whether each preregistered hypothesis is paired with its registered decision rule, margin, and fixed interpretation sentence and the Results apply them verbatim; whether variability (per-seed or per-fold spread, CI or range) accompanies every number a rule is judged against; whether confirmatory and exploratory (post-unblinding, pre-run-hashed addenda) results are labelled distinctly in Methods, Results, tables and Abstract; whether every deviation from the registration is listed with its inferential consequence; whether numbers quoted in text agree with the corresponding table cells; whether the parameter-matching control is actually demonstrated (reported counts) rather than asserted.
what_triggers_block: A registered decision rule is applied with a different margin, metric, direction, or comparison set than the one stated, or an interpretation sentence is altered in substance at the point of use; or a post-unblinding addendum is reported with confirmatory standing (no exploratory label, or label dropped in Abstract or Conclusions); or a rule-judged number appears without any variability measure and the verdict depends on it; or Methods omit an element (noise model, split, seed count, fold definition, matching procedure) without which the headline comparison cannot be reproduced; or a material undisclosed deviation from the registration is discoverable from the manuscript's own text.
what_triggers_warn: Variability is reported for some rule-judged quantities but not all, or is reported as a bare range without saying over what it was taken; a registered rule is applied correctly but the registered wording is paraphrased rather than quoted; confirmatory vs exploratory labelling is present in Results but inconsistent in tables, captions or Abstract; a text-vs-table discrepancy exists that does not flip any registered verdict; the parameter-matching control is described but the matched counts are not reported; a deviation is disclosed but its effect on inference is not stated; the pre-run hash or registration pointer is mentioned without a checkable locator.
what_triggers_fatal: The registered decision rule and the reported numbers, taken as printed in the manuscript, yield a different verdict from the one the manuscript states for a primary hypothesis, and the Abstract or Conclusions rest on the stated verdict; or the design cannot in principle discriminate the primary hypothesis (for example, the leave-one-reconstructor-out protocol as described trains on the held-out reconstructor, or the comparison arms differ in something other than the factor under test with no matched control).

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether the Abstract, Results and Discussion state the same verdict for each hypothesis; whether falsified or null outcomes are carried into Abstract and Conclusions with the same prominence as confirmed ones; whether exploratory addenda are used only as exploratory evidence in the argument; whether the title's question is answered by the design actually run and the answer stated is the one the registered rule gives; whether the Discussion's explanations for a result are flagged as post hoc where they are; whether the scope of the conclusions (reconstructors, noise levels, geometry, training regimes) matches what was varied; whether limitations acknowledged in the Discussion are not contradicted by stronger wording elsewhere.
what_triggers_block: The Abstract or Conclusions assert a verdict that the Results section's registered-rule outcome does not support, or omit a falsified primary hypothesis while reporting the confirmed ones; or an exploratory addendum result is used as the decisive evidence for a headline claim; or the conclusion generalises to conditions (reconstructors, noise, anatomy, training protocol) the study did not vary while the Methods show it did not; or a post hoc mechanistic explanation is presented as a tested finding.
what_triggers_warn: Verdict wording differs in strength between Abstract, Results and Discussion without contradicting the registered outcome; a null or falsified result appears in Results but is softened or deferred to a late Discussion paragraph; a post hoc explanation is offered without being labelled as such but is not load-bearing for the headline; the scope of a claim is slightly broader than the varied conditions but the Limitations section corrects it; the title's question and the stated answer are aligned but the answer is conditional on a qualifier that only appears in the body.
what_triggers_fatal: The central thesis is self-contradictory on the manuscript's own numbers (the same hypothesis is reported as both supported and falsified under the same rule in different sections), or the headline claim is logically independent of every registered hypothesis and every reported result, so that no part of the evidence bears on the conclusion.
