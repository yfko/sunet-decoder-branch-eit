## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor, mandatory, methodology seat only). From the adversary's chair this dimension asks whether the study as designed and executed could have produced the reported numbers for a reason other than the one the authors give. For a preregistered simulation study the attack surface is the gap between what was registered and what was run: seeds, data splits, parameter-matching procedure, noise and reconstructor settings, the exact statistic and its decision threshold, and whether every deviation is reported where a reader would look for it. I do not score D1; I note where an argument in my remit depends on a methodological premise the methodology seat must check.

D2 (domain_accuracy, mandatory, domain seat only). The adversary's version of this dimension is: does the manuscript describe the prior literature and the domain facts in a way that makes its own contribution look larger or safer than it is? Misstated prior results, terminology used with a shifted meaning, and domain claims that are asserted rather than cited are the typical entry points for a counter-narrative. I do not score D2; if a logical step in the central argument rests on a domain assertion I cannot verify, I flag the dependency rather than adjudicate the fact.

D3 (argumentative_coherence, mandatory, da and methodology eligible, da owner). This is my dimension. The question is whether the chain from preregistered hypothesis to registered decision rule to reported number to interpretation sentence to abstract claim holds at every link without a hidden swap. In a preregistered study the strongest attacks are: a registered rule applied with a different threshold or direction than written; a fixed interpretation sentence paraphrased in a way that changes its truth conditions; an exploratory or post-unblinding result given confirmatory standing in the abstract or conclusion; a number that differs between a table and the prose that cites it; a conclusion whose scope outruns the conditions actually simulated; or a deviation acknowledged in one section and silently absent where the affected result is stated. I also look for a counter-narrative that explains the same numbers more parsimoniously than the authors' account.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance, high priority, perspective seat only). Adversarially, the question is whether an engineer, a clinician, or a machine-learning reader from outside the subfield can reconstruct the claim and its limits from the manuscript alone, and whether any claim that reaches across disciplines is backed by evidence rather than by the authors' framing. I do not score D4.

D5 (writing_and_structure, normal priority, eic seat only). The adversary reads organisation, clarity, figures and tables, and venue conventions as places where ambiguity can hide a weak claim: a figure caption that says more than the figure shows, a table whose column labels do not match the text's description, or a Methods section that forces the reader to another document. I do not score D5; where such a defect changes what a claim means I report it under D3 as a coherence finding with a typed anchor.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution, mandatory, eic seat only). The adversary's reading is whether the paper claims a contribution that its own evidence does not establish at the configured venue's bar, which for this venue is technical soundness and data-supported conclusions rather than novelty. I do not score D6 and make no venue-alignment claim; I confine myself to whether the conclusions are supported by the data as presented.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: For each preregistered hypothesis, trace the registered decision rule (statistic, margin, direction, number of replicates or seeds) to the reported value in the table, to the prose sentence that states the verdict, to the interpretation sentence fixed in advance, and to the abstract and conclusion; check that the confirmatory and exploratory or post-unblinding addendum results are labelled with their standing at every mention; check that every number quoted in the text matches the table or figure it cites; check that the scope of the concluding claims does not exceed the simulated conditions, reconstructors, noise levels, and training regimes actually run; check that the Methods section is self-contained enough to evaluate each decision rule without an external document; check that every stated deviation from the registration is carried to the result it affects; and construct the most parsimonious rival explanation of the reported pattern and test whether the manuscript rules it out.
what_triggers_block: A registered decision rule is applied with a changed threshold, direction, or replicate count without a declared deviation, or a verdict sentence states a result that the cited table value does not support under the rule as written, or a post-unblinding or exploratory result is presented with confirmatory standing in the abstract or conclusions, or a concluding claim generalises beyond the conditions simulated in a way that the Discussion does not bound, or a rival explanation fits the reported numbers at least as well as the authors' account and is not addressed.
what_triggers_warn: A fixed interpretation sentence is paraphrased so that its emphasis shifts although its truth conditions do not, or a number in the prose differs from the table or figure it cites by an amount that does not change the verdict, or confirmatory and exploratory standing are correct in Results but blurred in one Discussion or abstract sentence, or a deviation is reported in Methods but not restated where the affected result appears, or the Methods omits a parameter needed to reproduce a decision rule that can nonetheless be recovered from a table or supplementary pointer.
what_triggers_fatal: The central conclusion of the manuscript depends on a verdict that the authors' own reported numbers contradict under the registered rule, or on a hypothesis whose registered rule was replaced after unblinding without being labelled as a deviation, such that no rewording of the Discussion can make the headline claim follow from the data as presented.
