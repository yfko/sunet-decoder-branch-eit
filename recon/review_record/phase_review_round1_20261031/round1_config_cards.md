## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor, mandatory, methodology seat only). From the editor's chair this dimension asks whether the study was built and reported well enough that a competent peer could trust the numbers: a design that answers the registered question, data handling and statistics reported to the field's standard, and enough reproducibility material (code, seeds, hashes, data) that the result could be regenerated. I do not score it; I only need to know that a block here from the methodology seat is a mandatory-dimension event the synthesizer will weigh above anything I report on structure.

D2 (domain_accuracy, mandatory, domain seat only). This dimension asks whether what the manuscript says about its own field is true: prior work represented as it actually stands, terminology used correctly, and reported results not contradicting established domain evidence. It belongs to the domain seat. For my oversight purpose it matters because a venue judged on technical soundness cannot carry a factual misstatement about the literature even when the experiment itself is sound.

D3 (argumentative_coherence, mandatory, devil's-advocate seat with methodology co-eligible). This dimension asks whether the paper's central line of reasoning holds together from question to conclusion: each claim rests on evidence actually presented, the confirmatory and exploratory parts are not silently merged into one story, and no fallacy carries the main inference. I do not score it, but the same evidence surfaces (abstract, conclusions, registered rules) that the DA reads for coherence are the ones I read for venue fit, so I expect overlap in anchors without overlap in verdicts.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance, high priority, perspective seat only). This dimension asks whether a reader from a neighbouring field can follow the framing, definitions and implications without insider knowledge, and whether any claim that reaches across disciplines is actually substantiated rather than gestured at. It is not mine to score. It is high-priority rather than mandatory, so a block here moves the decision to major revision but cannot by itself reject.

D5 (writing_and_structure, normal priority, my seat). This is the editor's reading-experience dimension: is the manuscript organised so that a reviewer and a reader can find the question, the registered rules, the results that answer them and the conclusions in the places the venue's conventions put them; is the exposition clear; do tables, figures and captions stand on their own and agree with the text; does the manuscript follow the venue's structural and referencing conventions. It is non-mandatory, so its worst outcome feeds F5 (minor revision) and never a fatal.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution, mandatory, my seat). This is the editor's gatekeeping dimension: does this manuscript belong at the configured venue and does it deliver a contribution that venue's readers can use. For a venue whose publication criteria are technical soundness and conclusions supported by the data rather than novelty or significance, "contribution" must be read as a sound, clearly scoped, honestly reported answer to a stated question, not as a claim of field-changing importance; demanding novelty here would apply the wrong venue's bar. The dimension is mandatory, so a block from me triggers F2 and a fatal triggers F1.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure
dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: IMRaD order with the registered hypotheses, decision rules and their outcomes locatable in predictable places; every number that appears in abstract or text matching the corresponding table or figure value; table and figure captions self-contained and consistent with how the text describes them; Vancouver citation numbering sequential and consistent between text and reference list; Methods written so a reader does not need an external preregistration file to understand what was run; main-text length and section inventory consistent with the venue's research-article conventions; deviations from the registered protocol reported in a findable place rather than scattered.
what_triggers_block: a numeric value stated in the abstract, text or figure caption that contradicts the value for the same quantity in a table or figure, or an organisation so disordered that the registered hypotheses and the results answering them cannot be located without reconstructing the study from fragments.
what_triggers_warn: captions that cannot be read without the surrounding text, citation numbering or reference-list formatting that departs from the venue's Vancouver convention in more than isolated instances, Methods that defer essential procedural detail to an external document, or a main text whose length clearly exceeds venue norms without a stated reason.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution
dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: whether the title, abstract and conclusions claim exactly what the registered decision rules and reported results support, no wider and no narrower; whether the confirmatory preregistered findings and the two post-unblinding addenda are kept in separate standing in the abstract and conclusions, with the addenda labelled as exploratory or hypothesis-generating; whether the fixed interpretation sentences that were registered are used as written when their trigger condition is met; whether the contribution offered to readers is a sound, scoped answer to the stated question, which is the configured venue's actual bar, rather than a novelty or significance claim; whether the manuscript addresses the venue's readership of practitioners who would reuse the method or avoid the pitfall it documents.
what_triggers_block: the abstract or conclusions present a post-unblinding addendum result with the same confirmatory standing as a preregistered result, or state a conclusion that is broader in scope (population, reconstructor, noise regime, task) than the registered decision rules and reported results license, or omit a registered fixed interpretation sentence whose trigger condition the reported results meet.
what_triggers_warn: Discussion-only overreach that the abstract and conclusions do not repeat, a venue-fit statement or contribution framing that leans on novelty or significance language the venue does not require and the data do not support, or a deviation from the registered protocol acknowledged in the text but not accounted for in how the conclusions are worded.
what_triggers_fatal: the headline claim carried by the title or abstract is the opposite of, or unsupported by, the outcome that the manuscript's own registered decision rule produces on the manuscript's own reported numbers, so that a technically-sound-and-supported venue could not publish the paper under its current title and abstract without the central claim being rewritten.

## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor, mandatory, my owned dimension). From the methodology seat this asks whether the study as designed could answer the question it poses and whether the written record lets a reader verify that it did. For a preregistered simulation study with post-unblinding addenda, the bar is: the registered decision rules and their fixed interpretation sentences are applied as written, confirmatory and exploratory results are kept in separate standing, the data-generation, training, evaluation and seed-handling procedures are specified in enough detail to be rerun, the statistics reported (point estimates, variability across seeds or folds, margins against which rules are judged) are the ones the rules require, and every deviation from the registration is disclosed and its effect on inference stated. Pre-run hashes and registration anchors are reproducibility affordances only if they are cited in a way a reader could check.

D2 (domain_accuracy, mandatory, not my seat). This dimension asks whether the manuscript's statements about the field, prior methods and prior results are correct and whether domain terminology and reported numbers are used accurately. I note it for completeness: where a domain misstatement would alter the methodological inference (for example, a mischaracterised baseline), I will flag it in prose for the domain seat rather than score it.

D3 (argumentative_coherence, mandatory, I am eligible alongside the DA). From a methods standpoint coherence means the chain from registered hypothesis to decision rule to observed numbers to interpretation sentence to abstract-level claim has no break: the conclusion drawn is the one the preregistered rule licenses given the numbers in the tables, exploratory addenda do not quietly upgrade into confirmatory evidence, falsified or null outcomes are carried into the Discussion and Abstract with the same weight as confirmed ones, and no claim in the title or conclusions outruns the design (for example, a generalisation to conditions the study did not vary).

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance, high priority, not my seat). This asks whether readers outside the immediate subfield can follow the framing and definitions and whether any claim of relevance to an adjacent field is actually supported. Not scored by me.

D5 (writing_and_structure, normal priority, not my seat). This asks whether the IMRaD organisation, exposition, tables and figures, and adherence to the venue's conventions are adequate. I will not score it, but internal numerical inconsistency between text and tables is a methods-integrity matter and falls under my D1/D3 remit regardless of where it is presented.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution, mandatory, not my seat). This asks whether the manuscript suits the configured venue and makes an original, appropriately significant contribution. Not scored by me; the dispatch brief states the venue judges soundness and data-supported conclusions rather than novelty, so my D1 and D3 scoring will not penalise a null or narrow result as such.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor

dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: Whether Methods are self-contained (simulation forward model and noise, data splits, architecture and parameter-matching procedure, training schedule, seeds, evaluation metrics, leave-one-out fold definition) so the study could be rerun; whether each preregistered hypothesis is paired with its registered decision rule, margin, and fixed interpretation sentence and the Results apply them verbatim; whether variability (per-seed or per-fold spread, CI or range) accompanies every number a rule is judged against; whether confirmatory and exploratory (post-unblinding, pre-run-hashed addenda) results are labelled distinctly in Methods, Results, tables and Abstract; whether every deviation from the registration is listed with its inferential consequence; whether numbers quoted in text agree with the corresponding table cells; whether the parameter-matching control is actually demonstrated (reported counts) rather than asserted.
what_triggers_block: A registered decision rule is applied with a different margin, metric, direction, or comparison set than the one stated, or an interpretation sentence is altered in substance at the point of use; or a post-unblinding addendum is reported with confirmatory standing (no exploratory label, or label dropped in Abstract or Conclusions); or a rule-judged number appears without any variability measure and the verdict depends on it; or Methods omit an element (noise model, split, seed count, fold definition, matching procedure) without which the headline comparison cannot be reproduced; or a material undisclosed deviation from the registration is discoverable from the manuscript's own text.
what_triggers_warn: Variability is reported for some rule-judged quantities but not all, or is reported as a bare range without saying over what it was taken; a registered rule is applied correctly but the registered wording is paraphrased rather than quoted; confirmatory vs exploratory labelling is present in Results but inconsistent in tables, captions or Abstract; a text-vs-table discrepancy exists that does not flip any registered verdict; the parameter-matching control is described but the matched counts are not reported; a deviation is disclosed but its effect on inference is not stated; the pre-run hash or registration pointer is mentioned without a checkable locator.
what_triggers_fatal: The registered decision rule and the reported numbers, taken as printed in the manuscript, yield a different verdict from the one the manuscript states for a primary hypothesis, and the Abstract or Conclusions rest on the stated verdict; or the design cannot in principle discriminate the primary hypothesis (for example, the leave-one-reconstructor-out protocol as described trains on the held-out reconstructor, or the comparison arms differ in something other than the factor under test with no matched control).

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether the Abstract, Results and Discussion state the same verdict for each hypothesis; whether falsified or null outcomes are carried into Abstract and Conclusions with the same prominence as confirmed ones; whether exploratory addenda are used only as exploratory evidence in the argument; whether the title's question is answered by the design actually run and the answer stated is the one the registered rule gives; whether the Discussion's explanations for a result are flagged as post hoc where they are; whether the scope of the conclusions (reconstructors, noise levels, geometry, training regimes) matches what was varied; whether limitations acknowledged in the Discussion are not contradicted by stronger wording elsewhere.
what_triggers_block: The Abstract or Conclusions assert a verdict that the Results section's registered-rule outcome does not support, or omit a falsified primary hypothesis while reporting the confirmed ones; or an exploratory addendum result is used as the decisive evidence for a headline claim; or the conclusion generalises to conditions (reconstructors, noise, anatomy, training protocol) the study did not vary while the Methods show it did not; or a post hoc mechanistic explanation is presented as a tested finding.
what_triggers_warn: Verdict wording differs in strength between Abstract, Results and Discussion without contradicting the registered outcome; a null or falsified result appears in Results but is softened or deferred to a late Discussion paragraph; a post hoc explanation is offered without being labelled as such but is not load-bearing for the headline; the scope of a claim is slightly broader than the varied conditions but the Limitations section corrects it; the title's question and the stated answer are aligned but the answer is conditional on a qualifier that only appears in the body.
what_triggers_fatal: The central thesis is self-contradictory on the manuscript's own numbers (the same hypothesis is reported as both supported and falsified under the same rule in different sections), or the headline claim is logically independent of every registered hypothesis and every reported result, so that no part of the evidence bears on the conclusion.

D1 (methodology_rigor, mandatory, owned by the methodology seat) asks whether the study design, the handling of data, the statistical reporting and the affordances for reproduction reach the bar a competent peer reviewer in this field would hold. From the domain seat I read it as the dimension that judges how the experiment was built and run; I do not score it, but I note that a domain-accuracy finding of mine may share a root cause with a D1 finding, and the two must still be reported as separate findings on separate dimensions rather than bundled.

D2 (domain_accuracy, mandatory, owned by me) asks whether the manuscript's claims are consistent with what the field currently knows, whether the prior work it cites is represented as those authors actually wrote it, and whether the field's terminology and the numerical results are stated without factual error. For a preregistered simulation study in electrical impedance tomography and deep-learning image separation, this means: the physics and reconstruction vocabulary is used in its accepted sense, the preregistered hypotheses and their fixed interpretation sentences are reported as registered, the confirmatory and exploratory standings of each result are kept apart and labelled, numbers quoted in the text agree with the tables they are drawn from, the scope of every conclusion stays within the conditions actually tested, and no prior study is cited for a result or position it does not contain.

I do not score this dimension; where I observe a domain misstatement that also breaks the argument, I report the misstatement under D2 and leave the coherence verdict to the eligible seats.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance, high priority, owned by the perspective seat) asks whether the framing, definitions and implications can be followed by a reader from an adjacent field and whether any interdisciplinary claim is backed. I do not score it; my remit is accuracy within the primary field, not accessibility outside it.

D5 (writing_and_structure, normal priority, owned by the editor-in-chief seat) asks whether the manuscript is organised clearly, whether figures and tables are of adequate quality, and whether it follows the venue's conventions. I do not score it; a table whose entries disagree with the text is a D2 accuracy matter for me only insofar as the disagreement is a factual inconsistency, not a presentational one.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution, mandatory, owned by the editor-in-chief seat) asks whether the manuscript suits the configured venue and makes an original, significant contribution appropriate to that readership. I do not score it; the venue described in the brief evaluates soundness and support of conclusions rather than novelty, so even my unscored observations on contribution should be read as context for D2 scope-of-claim judgements, not as a fit verdict.

## Scoring Plan

### D2: domain_accuracy
dimension_id: D2
what_to_look_for: Whether EIT and reconstruction terminology (forward model, reconstructor, cardiac and ventilation components, decoder branch, parameter matching, noise level in dB, leave-one-out training) is used in its accepted field sense and consistently throughout; whether each cited prior work is attributed only the result, method or position it actually reports, with no secondhand or inflated attribution; whether every preregistered hypothesis is reported with its registered decision rule and fixed interpretation sentence used as written, with any departure declared as a deviation; whether confirmatory results and the two post-unblinding addenda are labelled by their standing and never promoted to confirmatory status; whether every number quoted in Abstract, Results and Discussion matches the corresponding table or figure caption entry including sign, unit and precision; whether conclusions are confined to the reconstructors, noise conditions, geometries and training regimes actually tested; and whether the Methods are self-contained enough that a domain reader can tell what was simulated, trained and compared without consulting external files.
what_triggers_block: A headline conclusion in the Abstract or Discussion rests on a hypothesis whose registered decision rule or fixed interpretation sentence was altered, inverted or silently replaced without a declared deviation; or a preregistered hypothesis that fails its own rule is reported as supported; or a cited prior work is attributed a result, claim or method it does not contain and that attribution is load-bearing for the contribution; or a quantitative result stated in the text contradicts its own table in direction or magnitude; or a conclusion generalises beyond the tested reconstructors, noise conditions or training regimes without the limitation being stated.
what_triggers_warn: A domain term is used in a non-standard or shifting sense without definition; a prior work is paraphrased loosely so that its position is overstated or its scope is wider than the original; an exploratory or addendum result is discussed with confirmatory language in one passage while correctly labelled elsewhere; a number in the text differs from the table only in rounding, unit or precision without changing direction; a scope limitation is present but buried so that an Abstract reader would take the claim as broader than it is; or the Methods omit a detail a domain reader needs to interpret a reported quantity, while the detail is recoverable from a table or supplement.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript's central claim depends on a factual error that cannot be repaired by rewording: the preregistered decision rules, applied as written to the manuscript's own reported numbers, yield the opposite verdict from the one the manuscript states for the primary hypothesis; or the manuscript misreports the direction of its own primary result relative to its own table so that the title or Abstract asserts the reverse of what the data show; or the described reconstruction or separation task is physically or methodologically mis-specified in a way that makes the reported comparison meaningless within the field.

## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor, mandatory, owned by the methodology seat). Read from the outside, this dimension asks whether a competent reader from a neighbouring quantitative field could reproduce the study from the Methods alone and would accept how the data were generated, split, analysed and reported. For a preregistered simulation study this means the registered design and the executed design are the same design, and any gap between them is declared rather than discovered. I do not score it; I note only where an adjacent-field reader would be unable to tell what was done.

D2 (domain_accuracy, mandatory, owned by the domain seat). This dimension asks whether the manuscript's statements about its own field, about prior work, and about its own results are correct in the field's terms. From a cross-disciplinary stance the related concern is whether terms borrowed from imaging physics, physiology and machine learning are used with the meaning each source field gives them, so that a reader arriving from one of those fields is not misled by a familiar word used loosely. I do not score it.

D3 (argumentative_coherence, mandatory, owned by the devil's advocate with the methodology seat eligible). This dimension asks whether the thesis holds together: evidence actually bears on the claim, confirmatory and exploratory results are not silently merged, and no reasoning step is a fallacy that would collapse the central argument. My seat does not check internal contradictions or logical form; I only read for whether the argument, as presented, would be followable by someone outside the home discipline.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance, high priority, mine). This is the dimension I score. It asks two separable things. First, accessibility: can a reader from an adjacent field (a clinical physiologist, a medical-imaging physicist, a machine-learning engineer, a methodologist interested in preregistration) understand the framing, the definitions and the stated implications without having to fetch the registration document or supplementary material to decode them. Second, substantiation: where the manuscript claims that its finding matters beyond the immediate experiment (to practice, to other reconstruction or separation problems, to how such studies should be registered and run), is each such claim tied to a result actually reported and labelled with its confirmatory or exploratory standing. A claim of outward relevance that no reported analysis supports fails this dimension; a well-hedged, result-anchored implication passes it.

D5 (writing_and_structure, normal priority, owned by the editor-in-chief seat). This dimension covers organisation, clarity, figure and table quality, and conformance to the venue's conventions. Overlap with my dimension is limited to the case where structural choices (deferred definitions, scattered deviation reports) make the paper hard for an outside reader; I will attribute such findings to D4 only when the obstacle is one of understanding, not of style.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution, mandatory, owned by the editor-in-chief seat). This dimension asks whether the manuscript suits the configured venue and makes a contribution its readership would value. For a venue whose criteria are technical soundness and data-supported conclusions, contribution is a matter of the claims being earned, not of novelty. I make no venue-alignment judgement in this call because no criteria binding was supplied.

## Scoring Plan

### D4: cross_disciplinary_relevance
dimension_id: D4
what_to_look_for: Whether an adjacent-field reader can, from the main text alone, identify what signal was separated, what counts as success under each registered decision rule, and which results are confirmatory versus exploratory; whether every implication stated for practice, for other reconstruction or separation settings, or for study design points to a specific reported result and carries that result's confirmatory or exploratory label; whether field-specific terms and abbreviations are defined at first use with a meaning held constant across sections; whether deviations from the registration and the two post-unblinding addenda are explained in terms a reader without the registration document can follow.
what_triggers_block: An implication for clinical practice, for other reconstructors, architectures or separation tasks, or for how such studies should be designed is asserted in the Abstract or Discussion without being traceable to any reported analysis, or is stated with confirmatory force when the only supporting result is exploratory or comes from a post-unblinding addendum; or a central term is used with different meanings in different sections such that an outside reader cannot determine what was measured.
what_triggers_warn: Implications are tied to reported results but the confirmatory or exploratory standing is left for the reader to infer rather than stated alongside the implication; or domain-specific terms, abbreviations or the registered decision rules are introduced only in a table, figure caption or supplementary file rather than in the main text before first use; or a deviation from the registration or an addendum is reported in a form that requires the registration document itself to understand what changed and why.

## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor, mandatory, methodology seat only). From the adversary's chair this dimension asks whether the study as designed and executed could have produced the reported numbers for a reason other than the one the authors give. For a preregistered simulation study the attack surface is the gap between what was registered and what was run: seeds, data splits, parameter-matching procedure, noise and reconstructor settings, the exact statistic and its decision threshold, and whether every deviation is reported where a reader would look for it. I do not score D1; I note where an argument in my remit depends on a methodological premise the methodology seat must check.

D2 (domain_accuracy, mandatory, domain seat only). The adversary's version of this dimension is: does the manuscript describe the prior literature and the domain facts in a way that makes its own contribution look larger or safer than it is? Misstated prior results, terminology used with a shifted meaning, and domain claims that are asserted rather than cited are the typical entry points for a counter-narrative. I do not score D2; if a logical step in the central argument rests on a domain assertion I cannot verify, I flag the dependency rather than adjudicate the fact.

D3 (argumentative_coherence, mandatory, da and methodology eligible, da owner). This is my dimension. The question is whether the chain from preregistered hypothesis to registered decision rule to reported number to interpretation sentence to abstract claim holds at every link without a hidden swap. In a preregistered study the strongest attacks are: a registered rule applied with a different threshold or direction than written; a fixed interpretation sentence paraphrased in a way that changes its truth conditions; an exploratory or post-unblinding result given confirmatory standing in the abstract or conclusion; a number that differs between a table and the prose that cites it; a conclusion whose scope outruns the conditions actually simulated; or a deviation acknowledged in one section and silently absent where the affected result is stated. I also look for a counter-narrative that explains the same numbers more parsimoniously than the authors' account.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance, high priority, perspective seat only). Adversarially, the question is whether an engineer, a clinician, or a machine-learning reader from outside the subfield can reconstruct the claim and its limits from the manuscript alone, and whether any claim that reaches across disciplines is backed by evidence rather than by the authors' framing. I do not score D4.

D5 (writing_and_structure, normal priority, eic seat only). The adversary reads organisation, clarity, figures and tables, and venue conventions as places where ambiguity can hide a weak claim: a figure caption that says more than the figure shows, a table whose column labels do not match the text's description, or a Methods section that forces the reader to another document. I do not score D5; where such a defect changes what a claim means I report it under D3 as a coherence finding with a typed anchor.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution, mandatory, eic seat only). The adversary's reading is whether the paper claims a contribution that its own evidence does not establish at the configured venue's bar, which for this venue is technical soundness and data-supported conclusions rather than novelty. I do not score D6 and make no venue-alignment claim; I confine myself to whether the conclusions are supported by the data as presented.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: For each preregistered hypothesis, trace the registered decision rule (statistic, margin, direction, number of replicates or seeds) to the reported value in the table, to the prose sentence that states the verdict, to the interpretation sentence fixed in advance, and to the abstract and conclusion; check that the confirmatory and exploratory or post-unblinding addendum results are labelled with their standing at every mention; check that every number quoted in the text matches the table or figure it cites; check that the scope of the concluding claims does not exceed the simulated conditions, reconstructors, noise levels, and training regimes actually run; check that the Methods section is self-contained enough to evaluate each decision rule without an external document; check that every stated deviation from the registration is carried to the result it affects; and construct the most parsimonious rival explanation of the reported pattern and test whether the manuscript rules it out.
what_triggers_block: A registered decision rule is applied with a changed threshold, direction, or replicate count without a declared deviation, or a verdict sentence states a result that the cited table value does not support under the rule as written, or a post-unblinding or exploratory result is presented with confirmatory standing in the abstract or conclusions, or a concluding claim generalises beyond the conditions simulated in a way that the Discussion does not bound, or a rival explanation fits the reported numbers at least as well as the authors' account and is not addressed.
what_triggers_warn: A fixed interpretation sentence is paraphrased so that its emphasis shifts although its truth conditions do not, or a number in the prose differs from the table or figure it cites by an amount that does not change the verdict, or confirmatory and exploratory standing are correct in Results but blurred in one Discussion or abstract sentence, or a deviation is reported in Methods but not restated where the affected result appears, or the Methods omits a parameter needed to reproduce a decision rule that can nonetheless be recovered from a table or supplementary pointer.
what_triggers_fatal: The central conclusion of the manuscript depends on a verdict that the authors' own reported numbers contradict under the registered rule, or on a hypothesis whose registered rule was replaced after unblinding without being labelled as a deviation, such that no rewording of the Discussion can make the headline claim follow from the data as presented.
